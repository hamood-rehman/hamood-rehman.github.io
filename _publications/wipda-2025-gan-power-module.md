The module combines a multilayer substrate, embedded decoupling capacitors, and co-packaged gate drivers to cut parasitic inductance and support fast switching. The power-loop inductance is about 5.52 nH, roughly 40% lower than a conventional single-layer substrate design (9.1 nH). Thermal behavior at high current was evaluated with finite element analysis, and the design was evaluated experimentally.
