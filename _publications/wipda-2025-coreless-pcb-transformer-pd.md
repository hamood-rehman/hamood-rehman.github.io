Coreless PCB transformers are widely used in wireless power transfer stages for medium-voltage gate-driver supplies, where voltage insulation, coupling capacitance, efficiency, and size all matter. This paper measures DC partial discharge in a coreless PCB transformer using a high-frequency current transformer, and reports how the partial-discharge inception voltage depends on the air gap between the coils: the relationship is linear, with inception voltage rising as the gap increases.
