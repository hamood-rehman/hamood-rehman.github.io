The supply uses a GaN full-bridge inverter at 2.8 MHz, a PCB air-core transformer with a 12.7 mm gap, and full-bridge rectifiers producing +15 V / −5 V at 6 W. With the inverter, transformer, rectifier, and switching frequency held fixed, four compensation networks were compared on coupling capacitance, output stability during high dv/dt, and thermal behavior. The matching network and rectifier, not only the transformer gap and insulation, affect coupling capacitance and common-mode immunity; networks with fewer reactive elements were less susceptible to high dv/dt.
