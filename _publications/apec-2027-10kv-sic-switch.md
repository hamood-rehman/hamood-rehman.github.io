Series connection of lower-voltage SiC MOSFETs is a practical route to a switch above 10 kV, but only if the devices share voltage during fast switching transients. This paper presents a compact 10 kV switch that integrates the gate driver, a dynamic voltage-balancing circuit, and a multichannel isolated auxiliary supply on each stage. Balancing uses a low-voltage NPN BJT in the Kelvin-source path of each MOSFET, operated as a voltage-controlled resistor during the turn-off Miller plateau and set pulse-to-pulse by a PI loop on the sampled drain-source voltage. Because the BJT shares the gate driver's local rail, no extra isolated supply is needed for the balancing circuit.
