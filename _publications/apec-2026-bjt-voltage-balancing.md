The method samples each device's drain-source voltage and adjusts the conduction of a low-voltage BJT in its Kelvin-source path, regulating the turn-off switching speed without snubbers. Since the BJT emitter sits at the MOSFET source, the balancing circuit shares the gate driver's auxiliary supply. It was validated in simulation with four series-connected devices and experimentally with three 1.2 kV SiC MOSFETs at up to a 1.8 kV DC bus, where the measured imbalance was reduced to below 5%.
