Five Wolfspeed 3.3 kV SiC MOSFETs were characterized for threshold voltage, transconductance, and parasitic capacitances, then tested as series-connected pairs in double-pulse experiments from 1 to 4 kV and 10 to 40 A (448 measurements). A Gaussian-process surrogate fitted to the measurements (test R² ≈ 0.95) was used for Sobol and SHAP analysis. Imbalance is driven mainly by interactions between mismatches; C<sub>gs</sub> and V<sub>th</sub> mismatch are the dominant contributors, while g<sub>m</sub> mismatch had negligible effect under the tested conditions. The result gives a quantitative basis for device screening and gate-drive design in series stacks.
